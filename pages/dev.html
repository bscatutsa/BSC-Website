About the Website

UT San Antonio Business Student Council's Website was created in 2025 by our Chief Information Officer, Tony Zamudio. It continues to be managed by our current and future CIO's, alongside any noted collaborators that have extended their aid. The goal was to provide a professional, easy-to-navigate site for UT San Antonio Business Students; to learn how the organization can contribute to their development as business professionals.

Meet the Developer

Tony Zamudio was the CIO of the Business Student Council for the Spring of 2025 and the founder of our organization's website. He sought to increase recognition around the organization by creating a hub where like-minded students and staff could gain further understanding of the organization's mission.

Meet the Current CIO

Jon Jaramillo, is the current the Chief Information Officer of the Business Student Council for the Fall of 2025. Jon prides himself on his ability to learn new skills and adapt to new environments quickly. He is excited to continue the work started by Tony and the previous officers of the organization.

Want to Collaborate?

Have ideas to improve the BSC website? Reach out to the CIO or fill out our contact form to get involved with website updates at the Business Student Council! We’re always looking for innovative thinkers, designers, and developers who want to help enhance user experience, streamline information for members, and build new online features that showcase what BSC has to offer. Your input helps shape the next generation of our organization’s online presence!
